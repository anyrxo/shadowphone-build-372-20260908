Add a Phone

            
Plug the phone into this computer with a USB cable. The wizard runs each step automatically — you only need to tap prompts on the phone screen when they appear (USB debugging, Tailscale sign-in, VPN permission).


            

                
!

                

                    
Before you start — Tailscale on THIS computer

                    

                        Tailscale must already be installed + signed-in on this computer with the owner's Google account. Check the tray icon next to the clock — it should say "Connected". If not:

                        1. Download from tailscale.com/download

                        2. Install + launch → "Sign in with Google" → use the owner account

                        3. Once the tray icon shows "Connected", come back and continue.
                    

                

            

            

                
1

                

                    
Detect phone over USB

                    

                        On the phone (Owner profile only), first turn on Developer Options: Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times. Then go back → System → Developer options → flip USB debugging ON.


                        Plug the USB cable into this computer. When the phone shows "Allow USB debugging?", tick Always allow from this computer and tap Allow.
                    

                

            

            

                
2

                

                    
Install Tailscale on the phone

                    
Downloads the latest Tailscale APK and sideloads it over USB. If the sideload fails the wizard opens the Play Store on the phone — just tap Install.

                

            

            

                
3

                

                    
Sign in to Tailscale on the phone

                    

                        Opens the Tailscale app. Sign in with the same Google account that owns the tailnet (ask Anyro if you're not sure).


                        ⚠ GrapheneOS only — do this BEFORE you can sign in:

                        On the phone's Owner profile, open Settings → Network & Internet → VPN → tap the ⚙ gear next to Tailscale → toggle "Block connections without VPN" OFF. Without this, Tailscale's sign-in browser can't load and the app appears broken.


                        Then in Tailscale: tap Sign in → finish the browser flow. Android pops "Connection request" — tap OK to allow the VPN. Click Continue back in this wizard when you've signed in.
                    

                

            

            

                
4

                

                    
Wait for tailnet connection

                    
Watches the phone for up to 3 minutes until it gets a 100.x.x.x tailnet address. If it stalls: check the phone is connected to WiFi and Tailscale shows "Connected" at the top of its screen.

                

            

            

                
5

                

                    
Enable WiFi-ADB (adb tcpip 5555)

                    

                        Tells the phone to accept adb commands over tailnet. USB cable MUST be plugged in for this step — after this completes you can unplug it and the phone keeps working over Tailscale.


                        Heads up: wireless ADB resets every time the phone reboots — if a phone goes offline after a restart, plug USB back in and re-run this wizard to re-enable it.
                    

                

            

            

                
6

                

                    
Battery exemption for Tailscale

                    
Stops Android from killing Tailscale in the background. Without this the phone drops off the tailnet every few hours.

                

            

            

                
7

                

                    
Install ShadowPhone Companion

                    
Installs the Companion APK which auto-restores Wireless Debugging after every phone reboot — no more re-plugging USB when a phone comes back online.

                

            

            

                
✓

                

                    
Done — what happens next

                    

                        You can unplug the USB cable now. The phone talks to this computer over Tailscale from here on — no cable needed for normal use.


                        • Right-click ShadowPhone tray icon → see the phone listed → "Launch" to mirror it.

                        • Or click Refresh in this Fleet panel and the phone appears in the list.

                        • The phone auto-registers in the cloud — every VA signed into the same account sees it after they hit ☁ Sync (above) on their machine.


                        Re-pair after reboot: Android wipes tcpip 5555 on every restart. If a phone goes offline after a reboot, plug it back in via USB and run this wizard again — it'll re-enable wireless ADB.
